# Group drag and resize repair
User specifies ordinary drag moves the whole merged outer-frame group; stationary left hold for 2000ms pops out the active member. Movement >=4px cancels separation timer. Early release/cancel/blur cancels timer. Separation commits at hold threshold, while merging remains release-only. Group position is distinct from each member position/style/size. Resizing uses group display coordinates even while busy. Persist group movement without altering member profiles and retain local group geometry during state publication. Tests cover move, cancellation, short hold, long hold and resize at display position, and existing merge release semantics.
